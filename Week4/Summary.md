## 📙 Stakeholder Presentation Summary

The Power BI Sales Performance Dashboard analyzed overall sales, profit, and cost performance across products, countries, and months.

The business generated total sales of **₹118.73M**, with a total profit of **₹16.89M** and total Cost of Goods Sold (COGS) of **₹101.83M**. Since COGS accounts for a large portion of total sales, controlling product costs and maintaining suitable selling prices are important for improving profitability.

**Paseo** was the best-performing product. It generated **₹33.01M** in sales, contributing approximately **27.8%** of total sales, and produced the highest profit of **₹4.80M**. However, Paseo also recorded the highest COGS at **₹28.20M**. This means the product is a major revenue and profit contributor, but its cost structure should be monitored closely to protect margins.

The **United States of America** was the top-performing country, generating **₹25.03M** in sales. This market should be prioritized through customer-retention initiatives, product availability planning, and focused marketing campaigns.

Monthly analysis showed that sales were highest in **October**, reaching **₹21.70M**. In contrast, **January** recorded the lowest sales at **₹6.60M**. The business should examine what contributed to strong October performance and consider applying similar promotions, inventory planning, or sales strategies during lower-performing months.

The COGS comparison also highlights potential margin-related opportunities. While Paseo recorded the highest COGS, **Carretera** showed relatively tight profitability, with **₹12.00M** in COGS and only **₹1.80M** in profit. Carretera may require a review of manufacturing costs, selling price, discounts, or sales strategy to improve its margin.

## Recommendations

- Continue prioritizing **Paseo** because it is the largest contributor to sales and profit, while monitoring its high COGS to protect profitability.
- Focus sales and retention initiatives in the **United States of America**, the highest-performing country.
- Study the sales activities, discounts, demand patterns, and product performance during **October** to identify strategies that can be repeated in weaker months.
- Investigate the low sales performance in **January** and consider targeted promotions or demand-generation activities.
- Review the pricing, discounts, and production costs of **Carretera** to improve its profit margin.
